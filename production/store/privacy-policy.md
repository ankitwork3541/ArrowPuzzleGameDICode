# Privacy Policy — Arrow Dragon Puzzle

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how DiCode Infotech ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game
**Arrow Dragon Puzzle** (package `in.dicodeinfotech.arrowdragonpuzzle`) on Android.

## Information we collect

The game has no account, login or sign-up, and it never asks you to type in your name, email address, phone
number, photos or contacts.

Your game progress (levels cleared, hints left, sound and other settings) is stored only on your device.

The game uses the following third-party services from Google. These services collect some information
automatically:

| Service | What it collects | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) | Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), interactions with ads, and diagnostic information | To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud |
| Google Analytics for Firebase | An app instance ID, the Android advertising ID, approximate location derived from the IP address, device model and OS version, and in-game events (for example level started or completed and hints used) | To understand how the game is played, improve it, and measure our advertising campaigns |
| Google User Messaging Platform | Your consent choices | To ask for and store your privacy consent where the law requires it |

We do not sell your personal information.

## Advertising and consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before
personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time from **Settings > Privacy choices** in
the game.

You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings
(**Settings > Google > Ads**).

## How these services handle data

All data is sent over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS).

Google's handling of this data is described in:
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Data retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics, then deleted automatically.

To ask for deletion of analytics data linked to your device, email us at the address below. We will ask
Google to delete the data tied to your app instance.

Uninstalling the game removes all progress stored on your device.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from
them.
